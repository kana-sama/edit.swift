# Sidebar Menu

The sidebar header shows a semibold title with a downward chevron at its left edge in both regular windows and full screen. When the macOS window buttons are visible, the sidebar switcher sits immediately to their right. In full screen it returns to the left edge when the buttons hide. The sidebar title shares the leading alignment of the folder title shown when the sidebar is hidden. The title keeps that alignment across modes and while dragging the divider. The chevron has extra space after the title and uses the same secondary label color as unselected Worktree disclosures. The title is a native AppKit pop-up button that opens the sidebar switcher with the checked row positioned over the title, as in Finder’s window-title menu. The label keeps its semibold weight while the menu is open. Press and hold on the title, drag to a mode, and release to switch in one gesture. A click also opens the menu for a subsequent selection. The switcher lists Worktree, [Scratches](../scratch-files/spec.md#scratches-sidebar), Source Control, and Search in that order. Source Control is disabled, and the active sidebar is checked. Choosing Search focuses its query field and keeps the current query. [Worktree](../worktree/spec.md) is disabled until the window has an open folder.

Whenever the borderless ellipsis is visible, its button stays exactly 10 points from the sidebar’s right edge. This applies in regular windows and full screen, including while the window buttons appear or hide, when switching modes, during resizing, and on session restore. It opens the native menu for the active panel, aligned with the button's trailing edge. Search offers Refresh Search. Worktree offers New File and New Folder for the open folder, followed by Show Hidden Files. The ellipsis appears when the panel has actions. The sidebar's minimum width is 211 points so both controls fit beside the window buttons. The sidebar and its header open and close immediately in a window and in full screen. Command-B toggles the sidebar in its last mode. Command-F focuses Search, Shift-Command-E focuses Worktree, and Shift-Command-W focuses Scratches. These shortcuts switch to their panel and reveal the sidebar if hidden. Press the same shortcut while focus is in that panel to focus the current buffer and keep that panel visible.

## Acceptance

Open the sidebar and choose Search from the title menu. Verify that Search is checked, the query stays intact, and the field receives focus. Dismiss a result, then choose Refresh Search from the ellipsis menu to restore it. Close and reopen the sidebar. Check that the title and ellipsis appear and disappear immediately with the panel.

Switch between Search and Worktree in a window and in full screen. Verify that the menu opens with the checked row over the title for each mode, that press-drag-release switches modes, and that the ellipsis opens below its button and lists the current panel's actions. Drag the divider from a wide sidebar to its minimum width and back in each mode. Both controls stay visible. The title and its chevron stay in place, and the ellipsis follows the divider.

In full screen, move the pointer to the top edge to show the window buttons, then away to hide them. The sidebar switcher moves with the window buttons and the ellipsis stays at the right edge of the sidebar throughout. Both controls remain usable throughout the transition without opening toolbar overflow.

Quit with Worktree visible and relaunch Edit. Verify that the ellipsis is at the sidebar’s trailing edge as soon as the restored header appears.
